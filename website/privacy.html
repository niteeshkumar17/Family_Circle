Privacy Policy

Flockar is committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Introduction

Flockar Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Flockar mobile application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use the Service, and information from other sources.

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, and profile picture, which you voluntarily provide when registering with the Service.
  • Location Data: To provide our core services (location sharing and geofences), we require continuous access to your device's location data. If you grant us permission, we collect precise location data even when the application is closed or running in the background.
  • Device Information: We collect details about the device you use to access our Service, including device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, battery status, and mobile network information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve our services, including:

  • Displaying your real-time location to authorized members of your Nest groups.
  • Triggering smart notification alerts when you arrive at or leave safe zones (Geofences).
  • Monitoring battery and charging statuses to share within your private group.
  • Processing emergency SOS requests and alerting your Nest members immediately.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing security vulnerabilities or technical issues.

4. Sharing of Your Information

We do not sell your personal or location data to third parties. Your data is shared strictly in the following scenarios:

  • With Nest Members: Your real-time location, location history, battery status, and SOS events are shared only with the members of the Nest groups you have explicitly joined or invited.
  • Service Providers: We may share your information with third-party vendors who perform services for us (e.g., Firebase database infrastructure, notification delivery services) under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures designed to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your location data is transmitted securely via HTTPS and stored using modern encryption standards. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.
